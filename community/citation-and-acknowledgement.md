# Citation and Acknowledgement

Researchers who use BDC resources, services, tools, funding, or cloud credits are expected to appropriately acknowledge BDC’s contribution to their work. Acknowledgement and citation should be included in publications, preprints, posters, presentations, and other scholarly outputs resulting from the use of BDC.

Recipients of support through programs such as NHLBI BDC Pilot Funding Program or Cloud Credit Support Program are also expected to acknowledge that support in any resulting scholarly outputs.

Standard citation and acknowledgement language can be copied from below.

{% hint style="info" %}
**To cite BDC, use:**

National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, National Institutes of Health, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (2020). The NHLBI BioData Catalyst. Zenodo. [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3822858](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3822858)
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**To acknowledge BDC, use:**

The authors wish to acknowledge the contributions of the consortium working on the development of the NHLBI BioData Catalyst® (BDC) ecosystem.
{% endhint %}
